BUSINESS INSIGHTS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
=================================================================

1. ORDER DEMAND
The highest order volume occurred in 2017-11, with 7,544 orders.
The first month in the dataset is 2016-09 (4 orders).
The last month in the dataset is 2018-10 (4 orders).
The first and last months may cover incomplete periods. Their low counts should be checked before drawing demand conclusions.

2. DELIVERY PERFORMANCE
Of 96,470 eligible delivered orders, 7,826 were late (8.11%).
88,644 orders were delivered on time or early.
Recommendation: investigate late deliveries by destination, seller, and order period to identify where delays are concentrated.

3. GEOGRAPHICAL PERFORMANCE
AM had the highest average delivery time among states with at least 100 eligible orders (26.43 days).
SP had the lowest average delivery time among the same group (8.76 days).
Recommendation: review delivery routes, distance, seller locations, and carrier performance in slower destinations. The state comparison shows an association, not the cause of delays.

4. PRODUCT CATEGORY PERFORMANCE
health_beauty ranked first by recorded item revenue, at R$1,258,681.34.
watches_gifts ranked second by recorded item revenue, at R$1,205,005.68.
Recommendation: use category demand when planning inventory and fulfilment capacity, while considering seasonality and stock availability.

5. CUSTOMER REVIEW PATTERNS
The weighted average review score was 4.09 out of 5.
Five-star reviews: 57,328 (57.78%).
One-star reviews: 11,424 (11.51%).
Recommendation: examine low-score orders alongside delivery delays and review comments to identify possible service issues. Do not assume delivery delay is the only reason for low scores.

6. FREIGHT CHARGE INTERPRETATION
The dataset records freight charges paid or associated with orders. These values should not be treated as the logistics company's actual internal transportation costs or profit.
Recommendation: compare freight charges by destination, order weight, and product category to understand customer-facing shipping patterns.

7. PROJECT LIMITATIONS
- The dataset represents Brazilian e-commerce activity, not global logistics.
- The data covers a historical period and may not represent current operations.
- Some order timestamps and product measurements are missing.
- Date sequence inconsistencies were flagged and not automatically removed.
- Duplicate review IDs were retained for investigation.
- Freight charges do not provide actual carrier operating costs.
- The analysis identifies patterns and associations, not proven causes.
